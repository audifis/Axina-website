AXINA / Companies / Payroll

Payroll and its entries, in one place.

You enter the month's variable items. AXINA calculates the payslips and posts the salary and social charge entries to your accounts.

See pricing
Where this module stands
  • Payroll and payslips in SenegalPilot
  • Payroll entries in the accountsAvailable
  • Other OHADA countriesPlanned

What you do with it.

Employee records
Contracts, pay items, tax profiles, advances: everything needed to calculate correctly.
The month's variable items
Hours, bonuses, absences, advances: you enter what changes, the rest follows.
Payslips
Calculated under the country's parameters, ready to hand out.
Payroll entries
Posted payroll records its entries in your ledger, sealed like all others.
With your accounting firm
The firm prepares, reviews and signs off payroll in its work queue, under mandate.

How it works.

  1. You enter the variable items

    Bonuses, absences, overtime for the month.

  2. AXINA calculates

    Payslips and contributions under the country's parameters.

  3. Payroll is posted

    Salary and social charge entries go into your books.

Frequently asked questions.

In which countries is payroll available?

Payroll is in pilot in Senegal. Other OHADA countries will follow, one by one, with their own social and tax parameters.

Does payroll go into the accounts automatically?

Yes. Posted payroll creates its salary and social charge entries, sealed like every AXINA entry.

Can my firm handle my payroll?

Yes, under mandate. It prepares and reviews it in its work queue, then signs it off.
