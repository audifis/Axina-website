What you do with it.

The SYSCOHADA chart of accounts, already there
Classes 1 to 9 are built in. You don't create your accounts, you use them.
Entries that balance themselves
AXINA rejects an entry whose debits do not equal its credits. The mistake is stopped before it reaches the books.
Journals, general ledger, trial balance
Always up to date, filterable by period, available to your firm under mandate.
Balance sheet, income statement, cash flows
Financial statements are calculated from your entries, not from a separate spreadsheet.
A mistake is cancelled, not erased
A validated entry is corrected with a reversing entry, then the right entry. The history stays readable.
A close that really freezes
The year-end close locks the periods and posts the closing entries. What is closed no longer moves.

How it works.

  1. You record the transaction

    An invoice issued, a purchase, a receipt, a payroll: AXINA suggests the matching entry.

  2. AXINA checks and seals

    Balance, valid accounts, open period. Then the entry receives its DIP fingerprint, chained to the previous one.

  3. Your statements update

    Trial balance, balance sheet and income statement reflect the transaction straight away.

Frequently asked questions.

Does AXINA follow the revised SYSCOHADA?

Yes. The chart of accounts, journals and financial statements follow the revised SYSCOHADA, in force in the 17 OHADA countries. Each country's tax rules are set up country by country.

Can I change an entry that has already been validated?

No, and that is deliberate. A validated entry is corrected with a reversing entry and then the right entry. That is expected accounting practice, and it is what makes the history reliable.

Can my accountant work in my books?

Yes, from the Business plan, within the scope of the mandate you give it. Every action of its firm is named and dated.

Do I need to know accounting?

Not for day-to-day work: you record invoices and payments, AXINA derives the entries. For approval and tax returns, your firm remains responsible.
