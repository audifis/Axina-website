What AXINA stands on.

Six principles, the same for the company, its firm and its bank.

AXINA is an accounting evidence infrastructure for the OHADA area. Every business transaction becomes a SYSCOHADA entry, linked to its supporting document, time-stamped, then sealed by the DIP protocol: a SHA-256 fingerprint chained to the previous entry. A mistake is never erased. It is cancelled by a reversing entry that everyone can see. The OHADA Uniform Act on accounting law provides that properly kept accounts may be admitted in court. AXINA provides the regularity and the trail. The weight of the evidence is for the judge to assess.

Your data stays under your control. The AI is off by default; when you turn it on, it runs first on your own machine, and it only comments on figures that are already calculated, never writing to the ledger. The firm only enters the scope of its mandate, and each of its actions carries a name, a date and a capacity: its sign-off commits it, the trail protects it. Faced with a tax audit, the company answers document by document, entry by entry. The risk of reassessment goes down. It does not disappear, and we do not claim otherwise.

Data Integrity Protocol

DIP, the trust protocol.

The integrity protocol that makes your financial data verifiable by any institution, without asking it to take your word for it.

  1. Every entry is time-stamped and sealed.

    When the entry is recorded, AXINA computes a SHA-256 fingerprint of the entry and its lines.

  2. Every fingerprint is chained to the previous one.

    The ledger forms a chain: changing a past entry would break every link after it. Corrections go through a visible reversing entry.

  3. Every document becomes verifiable.

    Invoices and files carry a QR code. A bank, donor or partner checks them in seconds, without an AXINA account.

What to know before you start.

What is the Data Integrity Protocol (DIP)?

DIP is AXINA's integrity layer. It time-stamps each entry, computes its SHA-256 fingerprint and chains it to the previous one. Invoices and files carry a QR code that lets anyone check in seconds that they match the register. DIP establishes that the figures have not moved. It does not replace an accounting professional's sign-off.

Does AXINA replace the accountant or the firm?

No. AXINA is designed to work with accounting professionals. The firm remains responsible for accounting and tax approval, and its sign-off is named. AXINA automates repetitive tasks so the accountant can focus on advice.

Does AXINA follow SYSCOHADA?

AXINA is built for the OHADA area. The chart of accounts, journals and financial statements follow the revised SYSCOHADA in all 17 member countries. National tax rules are set up country by country.

Do banks accept AXINA files?

The decision always rests with the bank. AXINA files follow the formats and financial indicators it uses, and the QR code lets it check where the figures come from. A clean file makes the review easier; it does not guarantee approval.

Who can verify a DIP-sealed document?

Anyone. By scanning the QR code, a bank, investor, partner or authority sees the document's verification record, without an AXINA account.

Can my firm work on my file?

Yes, from the Business plan. You authorise your firm, set the scope of its mandate, and can revoke it. The firm pays for its own AXINA for Firms subscription; it does not replace yours.

How does the AI work?

It is off by default. You can use a local model on your computer, or your own API key. A cloud AI service is only possible with the company head's dated, revocable consent. In every case, the AI comments on figures that are already calculated and never writes to your accounts.

Is my data secure?

Your data is encrypted in transit, hosted on secure servers, and never shared without your permission. Only you, your firm under mandate and the people you authorise have access.

Does AXINA work offline?

AXINA works locally first. Compatible modules keep working offline and sync when the connection returns.

How do I get started?

Create your account on the free Essential plan, set up your company, and move to Business or Premium when you need to.

Can I change plan?

Yes, you can move to a higher plan at any time. Billing terms are set out in your subscription conditions.

Can I bring in my existing data?

Yes. AXINA imports your existing accounting data. Migration is a separate service, priced on volume and complexity.
