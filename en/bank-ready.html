Your business is not the problem. The way it is presented is.

Scattered figures

Balance sheets from different years, out-of-date statements, spreadsheets redone for the occasion. The account officer cannot tell where the figures come from.

No trace of where they come from

Without a link between the file and the accounts, the bank has to take your word for it, or ask for everything again.

A file put together once, by hand

Each new request starts from scratch, with the same mistakes and the same delays.

Three steps. You prepare nothing on the side.

  1. You work as usual.

    Every invoice, every expense, every movement is recorded in your OHADA accounts when it happens, and sealed.

  2. AXINA builds the file.

    Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement, general ledger, ratios: the file is produced from your entries, with your firm's sign-off when it has been given.

  3. You send it to your bank.

    The account officer receives a structured file with a QR code. They check where the figures come from themselves. The decision remains theirs.
