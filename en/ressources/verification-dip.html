AXINA / Resources / DIP verification

An AXINA document can be verified in seconds.

Bank, customer, donor, auditor: scan the QR code and compare the document with AXINA's sealed register. Free, no account needed.

Understand the DIP protocol

What the verification shows.

The verification record compares the document in your hands with what is recorded in the register.

  1. Scan the QR code

    With your phone's camera, or paste the link into the verification window.

  2. Compare

    Issuer, number, amount, issue date and payment status. They must match the document received.

  3. Read the fingerprint

    A sealed document shows its DIP fingerprint. A document recorded without sealing is flagged as such.

The document matches

The information shown is the register's. The document has not been changed since it was issued.

The document does not match

An amount or a date differs: the document you received is not the one that was recorded. Contact the issuer.

Document not recognised

The code is incomplete, the document was deleted by its issuer, or it does not come from AXINA.

Verification establishes that a document matches the register. It says nothing about the issuer's solvency or whether the transaction was proper.

Do I need an AXINA account to verify?

No. Verification is public and free. It is rate-limited to protect the register from automated requests.

Which documents carry a QR code?

Invoices issued from AXINA, and files produced by AXINA such as the bank file.

What does "DIP sealed" mean?

That the entry or document received a SHA-256 fingerprint chained to the previous one when it was recorded. Any later change would break the match.
